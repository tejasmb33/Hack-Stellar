# Visual reference study and applied decisions

No reference artwork, models, videos, fonts or layouts were copied into this project.

## Grail — https://grail-app.com
Inspected the loaded homepage and the first scroll chapter in Chromium. Its opening combines a very large headline, restrained navigation and a spatial field of objects. The next section uses a clear chapter marker, generous negative space and a focused text column.

Applied here: a strong primary event heading, small section indices, one dominant spatial subject, and quieter text-led sections instead of animating each content card.

## Spyker — https://spykercars.com
Inspected the loaded opening and material-detail section using the www host after an initial connection failure. The hero directs attention to a large dimensional subject with restrained text and actions; the detail section pairs material close-ups with a controlled editorial grid. Also read the creator's first-party explanation: https://www.thebrinkagency.com/stories/more-than-a-car-designing-the-spyker-lifestyle-online-1

Applied here: significantly increased celestial scale, layered warm lighting, clear text placement outside bright disk areas, a physical camera approach/pass/recede sequence, and consistent material treatment across content surfaces.

## Igloo — https://www.igloo.inc
Opened the live site in Chromium twice, including an extended loading wait. It remained at its gray loading presentation in this environment, so a complete live motion review could not be verified. The supplied reference brief calls for a single immersive world, depth layers, particles and directed transitions.

Applied from that supplied creative direction: one persistent scene, three world-space star depths, orbiting and inward-flowing dust, low-density gas, and a continuous reversible camera path. There is no section-level canvas replacement, visibility cutoff or opacity ramp. This note deliberately distinguishes verified first-hand viewing from the user's stated reference direction.

## Product priorities
The reading experience remains ordinary accessible HTML: native domain selection, search, exact full briefs, sponsor containment, readable people cards and direct approved registration links. No cinematic menu, scroll hijacking, loading gate or canvas-only event information was introduced.
