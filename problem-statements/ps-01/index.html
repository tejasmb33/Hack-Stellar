PS–01

Unified Social Media Publishing and Management Platform

SOURCE / CONTRIBUTORContributor to be announced

CATEGORIESWeb Development / Automation / Social Media Management

Problem Description

Small businesses and retail store owners frequently use multiple social media platforms, such as Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, and other digital channels, to promote their products, announce new arrivals, and advertise offers.

Currently, business owners must manually upload images, write captions, and publish posts separately on each platform. This repetitive process consumes time, increases manual effort, and makes it difficult to maintain consistent promotional content across different channels.

Problem Statement

Design and develop a centralized social media management application that enables business owners to create, manage, and publish promotional content across multiple social media platforms from a single interface.

The system should allow users to upload product images or promotional materials, enter captions, select their desired social media platforms, and initiate publishing through a unified workflow.

Expected Functionalities

  • A centralized dashboard for managing multiple social media accounts.
  • Uploading product images, promotional posters, and other marketing content.
  • Creating a common caption with the option to customize it for individual platforms.
  • Selecting multiple social media platforms for publishing.
  • Publishing content across supported platforms through a single action.
  • Viewing the publishing status and history of posts.
  • Handling platform-specific publishing requirements and failures.

Expected Outcome

A functional web or mobile application that reduces repetitive marketing tasks and simplifies social media content management for small businesses.

Note: Teams may use official platform APIs, supported integrations, or simulated publishing environments to demonstrate their solutions.
