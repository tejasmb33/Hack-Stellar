PS–11

Aquifer Commons: Smart Groundwater Management System

DOMAINSIoT / Agriculture

Problem Description

Farmers sharing groundwater resources often pump water independently, making it difficult to monitor total consumption and ensure fair distribution, resulting in excessive groundwater extraction.

Your Challenge

Develop a low-cost system that estimates borewell water usage without expensive flow meters, monitors village-level consumption and calculates fair water allocation among farmers.
