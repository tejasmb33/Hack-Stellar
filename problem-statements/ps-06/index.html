PS–06

AI-Based Customer Issue Prioritization and Management

DOMAINSAI / Machine Learning

Problem Description

Customer support teams receive numerous complaints and service requests. Manually identifying urgent issues and assigning them to appropriate teams can delay resolution.

Your Challenge

Develop an intelligent system that categorizes customer complaints by severity and business impact, detects duplicate complaints and recommends the appropriate resolution teams.
