PS–02

Smart Inventory and Stock Management System

DOMAINSWeb Development / Database Management / Retail Technology

Problem Description

Retail businesses often face difficulties maintaining accurate records of their available stock, newly arriving products, and sold items.

Many small businesses still depend on manual registers or spreadsheets to track inventory. This can result in inaccurate stock counts, missing product information, delayed updates, and difficulties identifying products that need restocking.

A centralized digital inventory management system can help store owners maintain accurate stock information and efficiently manage daily inventory operations.

Problem Statement

Design and develop a smart inventory management application that enables retail store owners to digitally track, update, and monitor their product stock using unique product identifiers, QR codes, or barcodes.

The application should maintain a centralized product database and automatically update inventory records whenever products are added, sold, or removed.

Expected Functionalities

  • A centralized dashboard displaying available products and stock quantities.
  • Adding new products with relevant details such as product name, category, price, quantity, and unique identification number.
  • Generating or scanning QR codes/barcodes to identify products.
  • Automatically updating stock quantities when existing products are scanned and inventory transactions are recorded.
  • Recording newly arriving stock and updating existing product quantities.
  • Tracking products sold, returned, or removed from inventory.
  • Identifying low-stock and out-of-stock products.
  • Searching and filtering inventory using product names, categories, or unique identifiers.

Expected Outcome

A functional inventory management website or mobile application that improves stock visibility, minimizes manual recordkeeping, and enables efficient inventory tracking for retail businesses.

Note: Teams may implement QR-code scanning, barcode scanning, or unique product identification mechanisms depending on their proposed architecture.
